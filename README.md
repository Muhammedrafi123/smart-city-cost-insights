# 🌆 Smart City Cost Insight
![Banner](https://i.postimg.cc/ZKPsXx7n/header-dark-(1).png)
A modern data-driven platform that helps citizens, researchers, planners, and governments understand the real cost of living and infrastructure expenses inside a smart city ecosystem.

## 🚀 Overview

Smart City Cost Insight is designed to visualize, analyze, and compare urban expenses across different sectors like:

- 🏠 Housing
- 🚗 Transportation
- ⚡ Utilities
- 🌐 Internet & Connectivity
- 🏥 Healthcare
- 🎓 Education
- 🍔 Food & Lifestyle
- 🏗 Infrastructure Spending
- 🌱 Sustainability Costs

The platform transforms complex city expense data into clean, understandable insights using modern analytics and visual dashboards.
